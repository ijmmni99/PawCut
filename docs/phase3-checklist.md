# Phase 3 — iPhone test checklist

Pass: add a saved track in ≤3 taps; it auto-fits the video and fades out.

- [ ] Open a project → **Music** → **Import** → pick an MP3/M4A from Files; row shows length + BPM
- [ ] Tap ▶ on the row to preview, ♥ to favourite, then **Use** (2 taps total) → music row on the timeline,
      ends with the video, fades out (~2s)
- [ ] ⋯ on the track → change part (drag window, snaps to beats), title, tags, mood
- [ ] Tap the music on the timeline → **Volume & fades**: volume, fade in/out, ducking −6/−12/−18
- [ ] Drag the music clip / its edges; edges snap to cuts, playhead and beats (tick marks)
- [ ] **SFX** → tap Whoosh (preview) → "Tap to add" → lands at the playhead; **Auto-SFX** adds whooshes on cuts
- [ ] **Record** → voiceover with level meter → plays back; music dips under your voice
- [ ] Clip with loud original sound (barking / talking): music dips under it, comes back after
- [ ] Select a video clip → **Volume** → mute → only music/SFX in preview
- [ ] With music on the timeline: **Auto-Cut** → "Cut on the beat" on → cuts land on beats, music refits
- [ ] Export → music, SFX, voice and video sound all in the file, fade at the end (Photos + TikTok)
- [ ] Music ⋯ → **Back up library** → .zip in Files; **Restore** brings tracks back
